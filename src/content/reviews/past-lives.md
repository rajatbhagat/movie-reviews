## The short version

Two childhood friends meet again in their thirties, and the film refuses to
turn that into a love triangle with a winner. Gentle, precise, and much sadder
than it lets on. Recommended if you have the patience for a movie that
whispers.

## What worked

The restraint. Every scene that a more conventional film would use for a
confrontation, this one uses for a conversation. Greta Lee carries the whole
thing in small reactions.

The writing understands that the ache is not about a person, it is about a
version of yourself you left behind. The final sidewalk scene earns its
silence.

## What didn't

The middle stretch in New York drifts, and Arthur — while written with unusual
generosity for this kind of story — is more of an idea than a person for most
of the runtime.

## Final thoughts

The kind of film that gets better a week later, when you notice you are still
thinking about it.
